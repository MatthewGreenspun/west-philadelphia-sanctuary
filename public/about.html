What’s a third space?

Not home, not work

Your first place is home, your second is work or class. A third space is somewhere else you can simply be — without needing a reason or a reservation.

Where community grows

Libraries, parks, cafés, rec centers and porches are where neighbors meet, friendships start, and people find support.
